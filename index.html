Our story

Nila means blue.

In Hindi and Sanskrit, nīla is the colour of deep water and open sky. We named our water after it as a promise: keep the water pure, and keep the planet blue while we do it.

So Nilara skips the plastic bottle. Every pour comes from a paper-based carton with a resealable cap. It is light to carry, easy to stack and simple to recycle. Same clean water, a lot less plastic.
